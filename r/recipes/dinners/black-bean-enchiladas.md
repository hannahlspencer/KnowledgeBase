# Black Bean Enchiladas

### Serves 8

### Ingredients

200g flour

300g dry black beans

2 red peppers

1 courgette

2 onions

10g olive oil

8 cloves garlic

100g coriander

500g passata

Chipotle pepper

100g cheddar

250g ricotta

Paprika

Cumin

Thyme

Bay leaf



### Method

* Soak the black beans overnight. Make ahead 8 tortillas with the 200g flour mixed with 100g water. Make sure the dough is kneaded smooth before rolling each one out and frying them lightly so that they're no longer floury but still very malleable
* Add the black beans to a large, deep pan with the onions, garlic, and bay leaf. Bring to a boil and simmer for around 2 hours
* Blend dd the paprika, cumin, thyme, and chipotle pepper with the passata
* Lightly fry the peppers and courgette with the stems of the coriander until browning
* Fill each tortilla with a mix of the beans and vegetables, then lay in a pyrex dish. Pour over the flavoured passata and make sure it's evenly distributed
* Blob the ricotta around the top of the pan, then top the rest with the cheddar
* Cook for 20 minutes
