# Pork Larb

### Serves 4

### Ingredients

Fresh mint

Fresh coriander

Fresh ginger

Spring onions

300g dry rice

500g pork mince

1 red pepper

1 red onion

Lemongrass puree

Fish sauce

Limes

### Method

* Separate the mint and coriander leaves from the stalks. Finely chop both along with the red onion and pepper, and peel and chop the ginger into matchsticks.
* Boil the rice
* Fry the pork mince, then remove and set to one side
* In the same pan fry the ginger, mint and coriander stalks, and lemongrass puree. Once it starts to catch, pour in 4 tbsp fish sauce and squeeze in the juice of one lime. Then readd the pork mince
* Stir in the rice spoon by spoon until folded together, then add in the raw pepper and onion
