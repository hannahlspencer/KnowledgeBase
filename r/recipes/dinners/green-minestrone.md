# Green minestrone

#### Serves 4

#### Ingredients

* 1 tbsp olive oil
* 2 celery sticks, diced
* 2 leeks, sliced
* 5 garlic cloves, minced
* 2 400g tins cannellini beans, drained
* 1 litre vegetable stock
* 75g dried pasta
* 1 tsp mixed herbs
* 1 tbsp fresh basil
* 100g frozen peas
* 110g asparagus
* 1 lemon
* 2 tbsp nutritional yeast



#### Method

* Add the oil to a pan on medium heat and soften the celery and leeks for around 15 minutes. Then add the garlic and fry gently.
* Add beans and season with salt and pepper, then pour the stock over. Simmer for 5 minutes and then add the pasta. Cook for around 10 minutes.
* Add peas, asparagus, and herbs. Cook until tender, then top with lemon zest and juice, salt, pepper, and chili.
