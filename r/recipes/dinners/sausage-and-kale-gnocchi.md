# Sausage and Kale Gnocchi

### Serves 4

### Ingredients

6 pork sausages

500g gnocchi

500ml chicken stock

100g chopped kale

40g grated parmesan

### Method

* Squeeze the sausages out of their skins into a large pan with olive oil and fry until crispy. Sprinkle with herbs, whatever you like. Remove.
* Put gnocchi in same pan. Fry lightly, then add stock and kale. Once everything's cooked stir through the parmesan and sausage meat.
