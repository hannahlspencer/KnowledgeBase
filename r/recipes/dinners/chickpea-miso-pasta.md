# Chickpea Miso Pasta

### Serves 4

### Ingredients

200g pasta

1 onion

200g asparagus

5 cloves garlic

1 courgette

480g chickpeas

3 tbsp white miso paste

Juice of 1 lemon

### Method

* Fry the onion until soft then add the asparagus stems, chopped.
* Add garlic, courgettes, and spear tops
* Boil pasta, saving one mug of pasta water
* Add half the chickpeas to the pan and blend the other half with miso paste, lemon juice, and 80ml of the pasta water
* Combine pasta and sauce in pan with veg, add more pasta water if needed
