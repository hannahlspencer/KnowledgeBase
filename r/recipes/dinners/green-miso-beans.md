# Green Miso Beans

### Serves 4

### Ingredients

2 large handfuls cavalo nero

2 leeks

3 tins butterbeans

6 cloves garlic

2 tbsp olive oil

1/2 cup soaked cashews

2 tbsp nutritional yeast

2 tbsp white miso paste

1 tsp coriander

1 tsp cumin

1 tsp sumac

Juice of 1 lemon

### Method

* Blanch the cavalo nero then add to blender with the cashews, nutritional yeast, 1 tbsp olive oil, miso, 2 garlic cloves, and lemon juice. Blend until smooth.
* Caramalise the leeks with salt until brown. Add the rest of the garlic and the spices, fry lightly, then add the beans and their tin juice.
* Stir through green mixture.
