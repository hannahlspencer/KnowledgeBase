# Salty sweet lime biscuits

Serves

### Ingredients

#### Lime biscuits

* 100g unsalted butter
* 30g extra virgin olive oil
* Zest of 1 lime
* 200g plain flour
* 2g sea salt
* 3g baking powder
* 1 egg
* 80g caster sugar

#### Salty sweet sprinkle

* 30g caster sugar
* 2g salt

#### Lime frosting

* Zest and juice of 1 lime
* 240g icing sugar
* 60g unsalted butter (soft and squidgy)
* 15g extra virgin olive oil
* 1g salt

### Method

* For the dough: melt the butter in the microwave, add oil and lime zest and then cool completely
* Mix flour, salt, and baking powder
* Whisk sugar and egg until it's pale yellow, around 30 secs. Whisk in cooled butter/oil.
* With a plastic spatula stir in the dry ingredients until the mixture forms a thick, mashed potato–like paste and the bowl has clean sides. Scrape into a piece of plastic wrap and chill for a minimum of 3 hours, or ideally overnight, before rolling out.
* Preheat oven to 130c, roll out dough, and stamp biscuit shapes out and pop them onto baking trays. Reroll up to 3 times to keep getting them. Bake for 40-50 minutes.
* Mix the salty sweet sprinkle in a bowl and sprinkle on top of the hot biscuits.
* Whisk all the lime frosting ingredients together and assemble
