# Scotch pancakes

Ingredients

* 1/2 tsp white wine vinegar
* 150ml milk
* 110g plain flour
* 1/2 tsp bicarb
* 1 egg
* 1 tbsp vegetable oil
* 1 tbsp golden syrup

Method

* Mix vinegar w/ room temp milk
* Mix flour and bicarb in a bowl
* Whisk egg, oil, and syrup in another bowl
* Add vinegary milk to wet ingredients, mix, then whisk into the dry
* Heat 1.5 tbsp batter per pancake when frying. Shouldn't need oil.
