# Hobnobs

### Makes 16

### Ingredients

* 75g butter
* 75g granulated sugar
* 1/2 tbsp golden syrup
* 75g wholemeal flour
* 150g oats
* 1/2 tsp bicarb

### Method

* Preheat to 160C
* Beat sugar and butter until fluffy. Add syrup and mix.
* Combine flour, oats, & bicarb in a bowl. Add a bit at a time to the wet mix.
* Put blobs into cookie cutters and squash down
* Bake for 10 mins
